# NOLA Cajun Kitchen concept site: design system

## Color (60 / 30 / 10)
| Token | Value | Role |
|---|---|---|
| --white | #f9f7f3 | 60%. Page background for the daytime chapters, text on blue |
| --blue | #14213d | 30%. Headings and body text on white; the night sections |
| --blue-deep | #0d1629 | Footer, phone order bar, street backdrop |
| --blue-soft | #4a5672 | Secondary text on white (contrast 7:1) |
| --mist | #c3cad9 | Secondary text on blue |
| --orange | #c8431b | 10%. Order buttons and the menu board's category strip only |
| --neon | #ff5a2e | The lit NOLA sign tubes (light, not paint) |

Rule: orange appears only where someone can order, or on the sign. Never on headings or decoration.

## Type
- Besley (Clarendon style), weight 650 for headings, 400 body at 17.5px, italic for notes.
- Board (Sofia Sans Extra Condensed 700, caps): menu board letters only.
- Yellowtail + Tilt Neon: the neon sign only.

## Imagery
- No frames, boxes or captions. Each chapter is a photo that fills half the screen edge to edge and stays put while its text scrolls; on phones it runs full width at 4:5.
- One full-width photo between chapters, with a slow drift.
- Food photos are Pexels stand-ins until NOLA's own dishes are shot.

## Components
- Primary: hot-sauce-label button (orange field, white keyline inside the edge, 3px radius).
- Everything else: underlined italic serif link.
- Phone: order bar pinned to the bottom edge.

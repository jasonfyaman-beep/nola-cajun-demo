It started in a hot dog stand.

Robert and Judy Nguyen opened NOLA in 2019, in a former hot dog stand on Route 12 in West Boylston. You ordered at the counter and ate at picnic tables out on the deck.

People kept coming back for the gumbo, so they opened a second kitchen in the Shrewsbury Marketplace. The original menu still hangs on the wall.
